Airline names, logos, and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
OpenFlightClaims is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any
airline.

Airline logos, when present, are shown **only for identification**. A record
can use:

- `logoStatus: "missing"` when no logo is displayed.
- `logoStatus: "candidate"` when a logo URL still needs review.
- `logoStatus: "verified"` when the logo URL and source have been checked.
- `logoStatus: "removed"` when a logo should not be displayed.

OpenFlightClaims does not claim ownership of airline marks. Airline
representatives can request removal or updates through GitHub.

can be removed or updated on request.
