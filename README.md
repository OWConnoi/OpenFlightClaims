OpenFlightClaims is a free, community-maintained directory of official airline claim, refund, compensation, complaint, and passenger-rights links.

Most passengers do not know where to file a claim directly with an airline. Search results are full of claims-management companies that charge fees. This project cuts through the noise and points people straight to the airline's own page.

## What you can do

- **Search** — find your airline and open its official claim link
- **Report** — tell us if a link is broken or outdated
- **Suggest** — share a better official link you found
- **Add** — help us list an airline that is missing

## Why this exists

- Airline claim links are hard to find
- Claims companies take a cut of your compensation
- This project points people directly to official airline pages
- Community can report broken links and suggest improvements

## For developers and AI agents

OpenFlightClaims exposes read-only JSON, Markdown, OpenAPI, and MCP endpoints. No auth required. See [docs/ai-access.md](docs/ai-access.md) and [docs/api.md](docs/api.md).
